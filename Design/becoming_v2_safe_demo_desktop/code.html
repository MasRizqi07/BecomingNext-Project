Message from Year 5

The Strategic Innovator

You were always so worried about things breaking. I remember that tightness in your chest when a process deviated from the plan.

I'm writing this from a place where we still build strong foundations, but we no longer fear the tremor. We learned that the strongest structures are designed to sway.

The turning point wasn't abandoning your love for systems. It was when you realized that innovation is just a system for navigating the unknown. When you started building frameworks for exploration rather than fortresses for protection, everything changed.

We take risks now. Not foolish ones, but calculated leaps off solid ground. Keep building, but remember to leave a door open to the wild.
