Privacy and AI Boundaries

"Reflection before prediction."

Privacy at the Core

At Becoming V2, we believe that true introspection requires absolute safety. Your reflections, narratives, and insights are the most intimate data points imaginable. They belong exclusively to you.

Our platform is engineered from the ground up to ensure that you retain full ownership of your data. Your answers belong solely to your authenticated account, invisible to other users, third-party trackers, and unauthorized systems.

Technical Architecture

We employ a defense-in-depth strategy to guarantee data isolation.

code

Developer-Honest Guarantee

  • Client Isolation: Each user session operates in a strict sandbox.
  • Firestore Security Rules: Owner-only access enforced at the database level. Queries without matching auth IDs are structurally rejected.
  • Server-Side Callable Functions: All sensitive operations bypass client logic entirely, executed via secure, authenticated cloud functions to prevent manipulation.

AI Integrity & Processing

Becoming V2 utilizes advanced AI to provide reflective insights, but never at the cost of your privacy.

gpp_bad

Zero Training Guarantee

Your personal reflections are NEVER used to train, fine-tune, or improve our models or any external models.

The processing pipeline is strictly stateless. We interface with the Gemini API using a rigid store: false configuration. Data is analyzed in real-time, the insight is returned to you, and the context window is immediately destroyed.

Retention & Deletion

You control the lifecycle of your data. We provide granular control over what is kept and what is discarded.

  • Micro-Deletion: Remove a single reflection or AI analysis instantly.
  • Account Cascade Deletion: Initiating an account deletion triggers a hard-delete cascade across all our databases. All reflections, analysis data, and personal identifiers are wiped permanently without a recovery window.
