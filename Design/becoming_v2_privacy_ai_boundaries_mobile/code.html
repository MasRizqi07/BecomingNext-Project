Your reflections are yours. We've built this sanctuary with absolute clarity on how your data is handled, stored, and protected from intrusive models.

Data Ownership Storage Architecture AI Processing Deletion Policy

Data Ownership

The core philosophy of Becoming V2 is that you own your narrative. We act solely as custodians of your digital sanctuary.

Your personal reflections, uploaded media, and historical timelines are explicitly your intellectual property. We do not claim ownership, nor do we license your data to third-party data brokers.

Storage Architecture

We employ a robust, segmented architecture to ensure your data is isolated and secure at rest and in transit.

database

Firestore & Callable Functions

All user data is stored within isolated Firestore environments. Data access is strictly mediated through authenticated Callable Functions, meaning direct client-to-database connections are prohibited by design.

AI Processing Pipeline

Artificial Intelligence is used exclusively as a tool for personal insight, not as a mechanism for harvesting behavioral data. Our pipeline is designed to be transient and secure.

memory

Stateless Processing

  • We utilize the Gemini API in a strictly stateless configuration.
  • Zero Training Retention: Your prompt inputs and reflections are never used to train foundational models.
  • Context windows are ephemeral and discarded immediately upon generation of your personal insights.

Deletion Policy

The right to be forgotten is absolute. When you choose to leave, your digital footprint goes with you.

delete_forever

Full Cascade Deletion

Initiating account deletion triggers a guaranteed cascading protocol. This process scrubs your profile, relational data, and all stored reflections from active databases within 72 hours, with backup purges completing within 30 days. No ghost profiles remain.
